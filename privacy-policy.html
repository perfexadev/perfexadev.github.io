Privacy Policy

App Name: Habit Tracker (com.perfexadev.habittracker)
Developer: PerfexaDev
Effective Date: November 24, 2026

PerfexaDev built the Habit Tracker mobile application as an ad-supported productivity app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your personal habit entries, to-do tasks, streak progress, calendar history, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal habit or task records on external developer servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Habit Tracker displays banner and optional rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location (used for regional ad relevance and fraud prevention)
  • App interactions, ad impressions, and diagnostic/crash performance logs

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads.

For more details on how Google handles data, please review: Google Privacy & Terms and How Google Uses Information from Apps.

3. Local Notifications

If you enable habit reminders, the app requests notification permission on your device to schedule local alerts. Notification schedules are processed directly on your device and are not sent to external servers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your habit and task records are stored locally on your device, you have full control over them. You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Habit Tracker is intended for general audiences (ages 13 and older) and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
